LoanBook

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

LoanBook is a private loan ledger: a single web page, at mpomowole.github.io/loanbook, that a lender uses to record borrowers, loans and repayments. It is a record-keeping tool for the lender only. Borrowers do not use it, and it does not lend money.

What LoanBook asks Google for

When you sign in with Google, LoanBook asks for one permission: access to the files LoanBook creates in your Google Drive (Google’s “drive.file” permission). LoanBook cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

With that permission, Google Drive also tells LoanBook your name and email address. LoanBook uses them to show who is signed in and to keep each Google account’s book separate on a shared device. If your account keeps its email address private from Drive, LoanBook asks Google for it separately.

Where your information is kept

  • Your book — borrowers, loans, payments and notes — is saved as a file called loanbook.json in your own Google Drive, with dated copies in a folder called LoanBook daily copies.
  • A copy is also kept in your browser’s storage on each device you use, so the book opens quickly and keeps working without a connection. So that reopening the page does not ask you to sign in again, your current Google sign-in is kept there too until it expires, within about an hour. Signing out removes both from that device.
  • When you import loans from a spreadsheet, the file is read inside your browser. The file itself is not uploaded anywhere; only the loans and payments you choose to import are added to your book.
  • LoanBook has no server and no database of its own. Your book goes only between your browser and your Google account. It is never sent to the people who built LoanBook or to anyone else — the one exception is the short reminder list described below, and only if you switch reminder emails on.

What LoanBook does not do

  • It has no advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell your information or share it with anyone, beyond the optional reminder list described below.
  • It does not use your Google data for anything except storing and showing your own book.

Optional reminder email

Reminder emails are off unless you switch them on in Settings → Reminder email. While they are on:

  • LoanBook sends the LoanBook reminder service only what the email needs: the payments still owed (borrower name, phone number, loan reference, due date and amount), the total still owed, and your reminder settings (email address, time, time zone, days of the week, what to list and how far ahead). It sends an update whenever these change.
  • The reminder service runs on Google Apps Script under the LoanBook developer’s Google account. It keeps only your latest list, uses it only to send your reminder emails, and sends the email from the developer’s Gmail account under the name “LoanBook”, with replies going to you.
  • To confirm a request really comes from you, LoanBook includes a short-lived Google sign-in token. The service checks it with Google and does not keep it.
  • When you switch reminder emails off, LoanBook tells the service to delete your list, and the emails stop.

Other services the page loads

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, a charting library from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare), and Google’s sign-in library. When you import an Excel file, it also loads a spreadsheet reader from SheetJS (cdn.sheetjs.com). These services receive the ordinary technical details that any web request carries, such as your IP address and browser type. They do not receive your book.

Google API Services User Data Policy

LoanBook’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping, exporting and deleting your data

  • You can download a full backup at any time from Settings → Backup file.
  • To delete your data, delete loanbook.json and the LoanBook daily copies folder from your Google Drive, then sign out of LoanBook on each device.
  • To withdraw LoanBook’s access to your Google account, use Settings → Disconnect this account, or remove LoanBook at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Borrowers’ information

The lender decides which borrower details to record in LoanBook and is responsible for handling them fairly and keeping them up to date.

Changes to this policy
